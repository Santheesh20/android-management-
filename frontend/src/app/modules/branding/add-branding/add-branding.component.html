The red ring (~128 px / 7% on each edge) is the unsafe zone — TV cropping or overscan can clip content there. The green-bordered centre is your safe area.

How Your Image Is Processed

  • Near 16:9 source → fills TV edge-to-edge in full quality (best result).
  • Other ratios (square, vertical, ultra-wide) → original kept sharp, sides auto-filled with a soft blurred extension of the same image. No black bars.

Upload Tips

  • Use 16:9 (1920×1080) for the cleanest, most premium TV look.
  • Don't place text or logos within ~7% of any edge — keep them in the safe area.
  • Use high-resolution artwork — minimum 1920 px wide. Low-res uploads will look soft on a TV.
  • Background art can extend to all four edges (bleed) — only the centre safe area is guaranteed visible.
  • Avoid pure-black edges (the soft-blur fallback blends better with continuous artwork).
